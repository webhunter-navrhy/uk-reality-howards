Description

Situated upon the ever-popular Edinburgh Road, this delightfully presented two bedroom terraced home offers an exceptional opportunity for buyers seeking a completely turn-key property, whether for permanent residence, investment or holiday accommodation alike. Beautifully maintained and previously utilised as a successful Air B’n’B, the property is offered to the market CHAIN FREE and presented to an immaculate standard throughout.

Upon entering, you are welcomed into a bright and elegant sitting room, beautifully decorated and finished to a high standard, creating a warm and inviting space to relax and unwind. The accommodation flows seamlessly through to a stylish open-plan kitchen/diner, perfectly designed for both everyday living and entertaining.

A particularly rare and valuable addition for properties upon this road is the inclusion of a ground floor cloakroom, adding further practicality and convenience to the home.

Heading upstairs, the property continues to impress with a generous double bedroom positioned to the front aspect, alongside a further double bedroom to the rear, benefitting from access through to a modern fitted bathroom suite, finished with contemporary styling.

Outside, the property boasts a quiet and private courtyard garden, predominantly laid to paving, offering the perfect low-maintenance retreat to enjoy the sunshine, relax with guests or indulge in al fresco dining during the warmer months.

A beautifully presented home in a convenient and sought-after location, ready to move straight into and enjoy from day one.
